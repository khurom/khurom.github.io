What I do

Forecasting for energy and commodity trading

Price direction and size forecasts, power demand forecasts and wholesale gas and power allocation forecasts, built with the traders and analysts who use them.

Credit-risk and collections decisioning

Models that choose the next best action for accounts in debt: payment propensity, selection-bias correction, vulnerability detection and constrained call optimisation.

Experimentation, ranking and recommendation

Learning-to-rank, recommender systems and churn models, with A/B tests designed to reach a decision in days rather than weeks.

Production ML engineering

Data pipelines (Airflow, DBT), containerised model services (Docker, FastAPI) and MLOps on GCP, AWS and Databricks. Work runs from research through to monitored production.

Selected work

Credit decision engine, Octopus Energy

End-to-end ML engine assigning the next best collections action to accounts in debt. Corrected selection bias in historical data, then A/B tested in production.

Intelligent contact, Kraken

Wholesale energy forecasts, Bulb

Tech lead in the wholesale trading team, building gas and power allocation forecasts and the data pipelines behind them.

Ranking, Deliveroo

Churn, Just Eat

About

Dr Khurom Kiyani has a PhD in statistical physics from the University of Edinburgh and an MSci in Physics from Imperial College London. He spent a decade as a research scientist at Warwick, Imperial and École Polytechnique, analysing spacecraft data on plasma turbulence. He has over 20 refereed publications.

Since 2016 he has worked as a data scientist and lead data scientist in energy, commodity trading, e-commerce and credit risk. His clients include Shell, Octopus Energy, Kraken, Bulb, Utilities Warehouse, Deliveroo, Just Eat and Gucci.

Mohicans Ltd is how he takes on that work: small, senior and hands-on, from the first model through to production.
